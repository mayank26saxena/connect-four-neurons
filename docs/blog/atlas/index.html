Neuron atlas · Connect Four

Every neuron of a Connect Four model

A small transformer learned Connect Four from move sequences alone. Because the game's rules give exact answers, every neuron here is described by what the board looks like when it fires, the concepts it matches, the columns it pushes, and the tactics it supports.

01Which part learns which tactic

Four tactics, each a set of positions with a known right answer. For every layer and attention head we replaced its output by its average and measured how much the tactic fell. The control is plain agreement with the teacher on random positions: a part that hurts the control as much as a tactic matters to the model in general, not to that tactic in particular.
