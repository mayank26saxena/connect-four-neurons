## Hypotheses
H1: ply counter: fires when board is nearly full (ply >= ~34), more when the side to move is the first player (even ply).
H2: secondary: which columns are full (center columns 3/4 filled vs a low column 2/1) and the last move column (0/5/6 seem more common among firing).
H3: tactical content (threats) - no sign from examples; immediate wins/must-blocks mixed.
